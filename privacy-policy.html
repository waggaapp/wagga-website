Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Wagga, operated by Ellie Apps, collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Wagga mobile application.

By using Wagga, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

Wagga is designed to allow dog owners to find dog parks and connect with other dogs at nearby dog parks.

Dog Profile Information

When you use Wagga, you may provide information about your dog, including:

  • Dog's name
  • Description
  • Gender
  • Size
  • Dog photo

This information is used to provide the app's social and dog-park features and may be visible to other Wagga users.

Location Information

Wagga uses your device's location while you are actively using the app to find nearby dog parks and create new dog parks.

Wagga does not collect your location in the background when you are not using the app.

Park Activity

Wagga stores information about park activity, which may include:

  • Dog-park check-ins
  • Planned arrival times
  • The dog park associated with your activity

This information is used to show relevant park activity to other users and provide the app's core functionality.

Messages

Wagga allows users to communicate with other users through private messages visible only to friends.

Messages are stored on our servers to provide the messaging service. Messages are retained only for a limited period of a few days and are then deleted.

Push Notifications

Wagga uses push notifications to provide app notifications, such as notifications relating to dog-park activity and messages.

To deliver push notifications, Wagga uses the push-notification services provided by the relevant mobile platform.

Information We Do Not Collect

Wagga does not require users to provide personal account information such as:

  • Name
  • Telephone number
  • Email address
  • Postal address

Wagga does not use advertising networks and does not sell user information.

Wagga does not use third-party analytics or crash-reporting services.

Wagga does not currently offer purchases or subscriptions.

Photo Storage

Dog profile photographs are stored using a cloud storage service.

Photos uploaded to Wagga may be accessed by the Wagga application and displayed to other Wagga users as part of the dog profile.

How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide and operate Wagga's features
  • Display dog profiles to other users
  • Find nearby dog parks
  • Show relevant dog-park activity
  • Process check-ins and planned arrivals
  • Enable communication between users
  • Send relevant push notifications
  • Maintain the security and functionality of the service
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements

We do not use the information we collect for advertising.

Information Sharing

Wagga does not sell personal information or share user information with advertising networks.

Information Visible to Other Users

Dog profile information is intentionally shared with other Wagga users as part of the app's functionality.

For example, other users may be able to see:

  • Your dog's profile information
  • Your dog's profile photograph
  • Information about your dog's presence or planned arrival at a dog park

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Wagga's services and for legitimate operational or legal purposes.

Private messages are retained only for a few days before being deleted.

Park activity information may be retained as necessary to provide the app's functionality.

Dog profile information is retained while the associated profile remains active.

Account and Data Deletion

Wagga does not currently provide an in-app account deletion function.

If you would like your account or associated information to be deleted, please contact us at wagga.app@gmail.com.

Children's Privacy

Wagga is not intended to be used by children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If we become aware that a child has provided personal information through the service, we may take appropriate steps to remove the information and address the associated account.

Wagga has a separate Child Safety & CSAE Policy prohibiting child sexual abuse and exploitation and related content and behavior.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through Wagga against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
