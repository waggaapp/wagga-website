Child Safety & CSAE Policy

Wagga has a zero-tolerance policy for Child Sexual Abuse and 1Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).

We strictly prohibit users from creating, uploading, requesting, sharing, distributing, or facilitating content or behavior that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers children.

Wagga does not permit the use of its services for grooming, sexual exploitation of children, or any other form of child sexual abuse.

Violations of this policy may result in immediate content removal and suspension or permanent termination of the offending account.

Where required by applicable law, suspected or confirmed CSAM and other serious child-safety violations may be reported to the appropriate authorities or relevant child-safety organizations.

Users can report suspected child-safety violations directly through the reporting functionality in the Wagga app or by contacting wagga.app@gmail.com.

Wagga is committed to maintaining a safe environment for all users and to complying with applicable child-safety laws and regulations.
